⇢LOGISTICS ORCHESTRATION08

One trip. Many moving parts.
A clear state for each.

THE CHALLENGE
A freight marketplace needed to coordinate customer requests, vendor bids, dispatch, delivery, and settlement across a largely manual operation. Operations teams selected vendors using bid pricing and vendor credibility.
WHAT I BUILT
Designed and built the trip lifecycle as a state machine, connecting each stage to its required actions: vendor notifications, advance-payment links, waybill registration, and third-party vehicle tracking.
WHAT IT ENABLED
A connected workflow from request to trip closure, with explicit stages and automated actions supporting the operations team’s decisions.
